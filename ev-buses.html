Notes, sources and limits

  • IEA numbers. Electric bus sales, stock (buses on the road) and shares: IEA Global EV Data Explorer, Global EV Outlook 2026 (CC BY 4.0), pulled from its public API. Electric = battery-electric + plug-in hybrid + fuel cell, all bus types (city, intercity, coaches, minibuses). The IEA rounds to two or three significant digits, so small countries can show the same figure in two years and shares are approximate.
  • City buses. Transport & Environment (T&E, Feb 2026), built on DVV Media's Alternative Drivelines for City Buses 2025: shares of new city bus registrations by powertrain for 29 European countries, read from T&E's published charts (a few thin, unlabelled slivers are estimated and marked ≈). Hybrids are not zero-emission. Edition and numbers are in assets/city-bus-data.js.
  • 2026 so far. DVV Media Group (battery-electric buses above 8 tonnes in the EU27, UK, Iceland, Norway and Switzerland; city bus segment counted separately), ACEA (EU27 buses and coaches, electrically-chargeable = battery-electric + plug-in hybrid + fuel cell) and the ICCT (EU zero-emission buses and coaches) measure different things and do not add up. Numbers are in assets/bus-2026-data.js.
  • ICCT. Zero-emission buses sold per country (battery-electric and fuel cell), from the ICCT Global ZE Market Monitor (34 countries including the United States, India, Brazil and Mexico), a separate count from the IEA's.
  • Fleet. "Share of fleet" is the IEA's electric share of all buses on the road. China's electric buses are mostly city buses, so its fleet share is far higher than anywhere else.
  • Trucks and vans. They have their own page: EV Trucking.
  • Sources: IEA Global EV Outlook 2026 · Transport & Environment city bus data (DVV Media), via Sustainable Bus, Feb 2026 · ICCT Global EV Market Monitor.
Electric bus data: IEA Global EV Data Explorer (CC BY 4.0). City buses: Transport & Environment / DVV Media. Zero-emission bus sales: ICCT.
