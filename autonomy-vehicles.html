The EU vote on FSD (Supervised)

An EU-wide approval needs a qualified majority in the technical committee (TCMV): at least 15 of the 27 states that together hold at least 65% of the EU population; four states with more than 35% of the population can block it. The next meeting is in December 2026; Germany's ministry says a vote is "envisaged"; the Dutch RDW says the EU procedure is still running and a vote is expected at that meeting at the earliest, not confirmed. We found no published day. Click a country on the map (or a row) to set its vote and see whether the bloc gets there.
