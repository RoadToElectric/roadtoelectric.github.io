How these numbers are built — read before quoting them
  1. The calculation. Yearly cost of each car = distance × consumption × energy price, plus the optional items you tick: insurance and other yearly costs (your amounts), tires (a set every tire life km for the gas car, every tire life ÷ (1 + extra wear %) km for the EV) and oil changes (one every x km for the gas car). The purchase price is paid once. Cost after N years = purchase price + N × yearly cost, at today's prices: no financing, resale value, incentives or price changes over the years. Break-even = extra purchase price of the EV ÷ what it saves each year.
  2. Canada and the United States use the data of the Electricity & Gas Costs page: gasoline 2026 averages (Statistics Canada cities; EIA, with 41 US states estimated from their EIA region and AAA state prices), home electricity (EIA residential average Jan–Aug 2026; Canada Energy Regulator modelled price at 1,000 kWh/month) plus the sales tax on the electricity bill. Car ratings: US EPA (fueleconomy.gov) and Natural Resources Canada, model years 2024–2027, combined cycle, energy measured from the wall.
  3. Europe, electricity: Eurostat household price (consumption band 2,500–4,999 kWh a year), all taxes and levies included, first half of 2026, for the EU countries, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, the Western Balkans, Moldova, Georgia and Türkiye. The United Kingdom: Ofgem price cap for October–December 2026 (Direct Debit average 26.32p/kWh plus a 54.83p/day standing charge spread over 2,700 kWh a year; electricity is zero-rated for VAT in that period). Switzerland: ElCom median 2026 tariff for a typical household (27.7 Rp/kWh) + 8.1% VAT. Ukraine: the regulated household tariff of 4.32 UAH/kWh.
  4. Europe, gasoline: EU countries: the European Commission's Weekly Oil Bulletin, Euro-super 95 with all taxes, average of every week of 2026 (). Countries outside the EU: one reading of globalpetrolprices.com (9 November 2026, Octane-95), converted at that day's exchange rate and scaled to the 2026 average with the EU's own Jan–Nov / 9 Nov ratio; checked against the EU countries that both sources cover, that site's level matches the Oil Bulletin within a few percent on average. Liechtenstein uses the Swiss price. Kosovo: no gasoline price found, enter one.
  5. Europe, cars: every electric model and every petrol and petrol-hybrid version registered new in 2025 in the EU, Iceland and Norway (European Environment Agency CO2 monitoring data, 2025 provisional; electric models with 100+ and petrol versions with 200+ registrations), with the registration-weighted average WLTP consumption. WLTP electric consumption is measured at the plug, so charging losses are included, but WLTP figures are 10–20% more optimistic than the US EPA cycle, and both are lower than winter or motorway driving. A few registry entries with impossible values were left out.
  6. Currencies: Bank of Canada 2026 average rates (1 USD = CAD = EUR). British pounds, Swiss francs and Ukrainian hryvnias were converted to euros at 2026 average rates.
  7. Rebuild: scripts/gas_elec/build_gas_elec.py, build_ev_models.py, build_ice_models.py (North America) and build_europe.py (Europe; the EEA extract is refreshed with src/europe/eea_pull.py).
