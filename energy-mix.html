How these numbers are built — read before quoting them
  1. Source: International Energy Agency (IEA), Energy Statistics Data Browser — World Energy Balances (supply, final consumption) and Electricity Information (generation), licence CC BY 4.0, downloaded 2026-11-10. 163 areas: the world, 11 regional aggregates and 151 countries and territories. Years run 1990 to 2025; the latest year (2025) exists only for the 48 countries and regions that have already reported it, the others stop in 2024 and are shown up to their last year.
  2. Total energy supply (TES) = production + imports − exports ± stock changes, in petajoules (1 EJ = 1000 PJ). It counts primary energy: a nuclear or fossil power plant counts for the fuel it burns, wind and solar for the electricity they produce (so a TWh of fossil power weighs about 2.5 times more in supply than a TWh of wind). Use the electricity section to compare grids.
  3. Final consumption (TFC) is what end users buy: oil products for transport and industry, gas and electricity for buildings, heat, and so on. Electricity's share of final consumption measures how far each country has electrified; EVs are one of the levers that push it up.
  4. Definitions used on this page. Renewables = hydropower, wind, solar (PV and thermal), bioenergy and waste, geothermal, tide. Low-carbon = renewables + nuclear. Fossil = coal, oil, natural gas. “Other sources” (the IEA's own catch-all, mostly non-renewable waste and heat) is shown but counted in none of these. Bioenergy and waste count as renewable although a part of waste is not.
  5. Cars and the grid (section 5): BEV share of new-car sales is this site's own series (annual, latest full year from 2024 on; projected years are not used). A country appears only if both series exist. The bubble size is the electricity generated. This compares markets, it does not measure the emissions of one car: that depends on the hours the car is charged and on the marginal power plant.
  6. Regional aggregates follow the IEA's own definitions (for example “Asia (excl. China)”, “OECD Americas”); they are shown beside countries but are not added to them.
  7. Rebuild: scripts/energy/build_energy_mix.py reads the three IEA downloads kept in scripts/energy/ and writes assets/energy-mix-data.js. The IEA publishes a new year every spring.
