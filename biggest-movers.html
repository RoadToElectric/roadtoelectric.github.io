Biggest Movers

Which markets are growing their battery-electric (BEV) sales fastest — by year-over-year %, month-over-month %, and by the raw number of extra BEV units sold. Each country is measured against its own most recent real data, so a country with an older or gappier source simply doesn't show up until it publishes again.
