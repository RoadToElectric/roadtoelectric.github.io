Privacy Policy

What this website and our YouTube upload tool do with data.

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Road to Electric is an independent project: this website (roadtoelectric.github.io) and the YouTube channel Road to Electric. This page explains what data the website and our YouTube upload tool use. Contact: roadtoelectric@gmail.com.

This project uses YouTube API Services. Our upload tool, “RoadToElectric uploader”, uses the YouTube Data API to publish videos to our own YouTube channel. Google's use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy, and YouTube content is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service.

1. The website

  • We count page views to learn which pages are useful: the page opened, the country of the visit (worked out when the page is requested), desktop or mobile view, and two dates kept in your own browser (first and last visit).
  • We never keep your IP address, never set advertising cookies and never build a profile of you. Only totals are stored.
  • You can switch the counting off on the Contact page (this also deletes the two dates), or clear your browser data at any time.

2. YouTube API Services: our upload tool

“RoadToElectric uploader” is a private command-line tool, used only by the owner of the Road to Electric channel. It is not offered to the public and no other person signs in to it.

  • What it accesses: only our own YouTube channel, through Google sign-in (OAuth 2.0), with permission to upload videos and to manage our own videos (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, status).
  • What it stores: one sign-in token, kept on the owner's computer, so that each upload does not need a new sign-in. It does not collect, store or process any data about other YouTube users or viewers.
  • What it shares: nothing. No YouTube data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Revoking access: access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Deletion: when access is revoked, or on request by email to roadtoelectric@gmail.com, the stored token is deleted within 7 days. API data is never kept longer than 30 days.

3. Cookies

This website sets no cookies. Links to youtube.com, and any embedded YouTube video, are subject to YouTube's own cookies as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Changes and contact

We will update this page if anything changes, with a new date at the top. Questions or deletion requests: roadtoelectric@gmail.com.
